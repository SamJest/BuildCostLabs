Practical answers

Short answers for the decisions that usually come up before ordering materials or sending a quote request.

Is pipe bedding the same as french drain gravel?

No. Bedding supports the pipe; gravel surround or french-drain fill may be a larger volume around it.

What should I measure before pricing drainage?

Measure the trench run, bedding zone, gravel zone, membrane coverage, and fitting or chamber count.

What is the easiest drainage item to miss?

Fittings and chambers are often missed because the first estimate starts with the straight pipe run instead of the actual trench route.
