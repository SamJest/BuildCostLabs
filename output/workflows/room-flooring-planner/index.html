Practical answers

Short answers for the decisions that usually come up before ordering materials or sending a quote request.

How many flooring boxes should I buy?

Measure the room area, add layout waste, divide by pack coverage, and round up to whole boxes or packs.

Should underlay be estimated separately?

Yes. Underlay rolls or packs rarely match the flooring pack count exactly.

Should I keep a spare flooring pack?

Often yes, especially for laminate or vinyl plank where batch matching and future repairs can be difficult after the product range changes.
