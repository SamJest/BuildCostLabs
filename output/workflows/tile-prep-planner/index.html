Practical answers

Short answers for the decisions that usually come up before ordering materials or sending a quote request.

What should I calculate after tiles?

Check backer board, adhesive, grout, trim, and cost before treating the tile order as complete.

Why use a tile prep workflow?

It keeps substrate prep and finishing materials visible, which makes quotes easier to compare.

What makes tile adhesive estimates unreliable?

Adhesive use changes with tile size, trowel notch, substrate flatness, bed depth, and product coverage, so the bag count should be checked against the actual system.
